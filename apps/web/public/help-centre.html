How can we help?

Find answers for calls, chats, accounts, linked devices, wallpapers and common connection problems. If you still need help, create a support ticket below.

☎ Calls

Voice/video calls show ringing until accepted. Offline contacts are reported immediately. The timer starts after the call connects.

💬 Chats

Messages remain in your conversation history. Realtime typing and read states depend on the server connection.

🔗 Linked devices

Use Linked devices to review active sessions. Sign in on another trusted device and review the new session from your account.

🎨 Wallpapers

Open a chat menu and choose Chat theme & wallpaper. Your selected wallpaper is remembered separately for each chat.

🔐 Account

Use Settings for password, two-step verification, sessions and privacy controls.

🌐 Connection

If the account service is unavailable, verify that the app has the configured production API URL and that the server health endpoint is reachable.

Popular answers

Why does a call say “Ringing”?

The recipient is online and has received the call notification. The state stays ringing until they accept or reject it. Once media negotiation succeeds, the connected timer starts.

Why does a call say “User is offline”?

The realtime service could not find an active session for the recipient. Try again when the contact is online.

Where did my old chats go?

Pro Chat does not intentionally auto-clear conversations. Keep the same account and production API/database so the server can return the saved message history.

How do I change a wallpaper?

Open a one-to-one chat, select More, then Chat theme & wallpaper. Pick any available wallpaper and press Done.
